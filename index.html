Rolfing® Structural Integration

A holistic system of soft tissue manipulation and movement education that organizes the whole body in gravity. Rolfing® addresses the body's connective tissue (fascia) to release, realign and balance the whole body, potentially resolving discomfort, reducing compensations, and alleviating pain.

Through a series of sessions, we work systematically to bring your body into better alignment, improving posture, flexibility, and overall well-being.

Rolfing® Structural Integration was developed by Dr. Ida Rolf more than fifty years ago. This therapy helps realign and balance the body by working with the connective tissues (fascia) that surround and support everything inside us: muscles, bones, nerves, and organs.

Orthopedic Massage Therapy

A medically-oriented form of massage that focuses on the treatment of pain and injury resulting from musculoskeletal imbalances, soft tissue dysfunction, and postoperative rehabilitation.

Using specific protocols, orthopedic massage addresses the underlying causes of pain by assessing and treating the affected muscles, tendons, ligaments, and fascia. This approach helps restore function, reduce pain, and improve mobility.

What is Orthopedic Massage?

As compared to a luxury or "spa-style" massage, orthopedic massage is a more medical and therapeutic treatment using a combination of techniques such as myofascial release, sports massage, and neuromuscular therapy. While not necessarily "deep tissue," orthopedic massage often feels deeper than traditional massage as it is working directly with the nervous system to release tight muscles and tissue.

How is Orthopedic Massage different from Rolfing®?

Orthopedic massage is recommended for those on the fence about Rolfing or seeking a more traditional massage therapy treatment. While similar to Rolfing in the use of myofascial techniques, orthopedic massage is tailored more to address a specific issue in the short-term, whereas Rolfing addresses the entire system, including a movement education component, over the course of the Ten Series.

Orthopedic Massage

"Fix me now" treatment — targeted, short-term relief for specific issues

Rolfing® Structural Integration

"Help me fix myself so I don't need to come in as often" — whole-body, long-term transformation

Both treatments are complementary to each other. Many clients start with orthopedic massage for immediate relief and transition to Rolfing for lasting structural change, or use both approaches as needed throughout their wellness journey.

About Me & My Approach

Vince Milosevich is a Certified Rolfer® and Licensed Massage Therapist formerly practicing in New York City, Santa Fe, NM, and now serving Narberth and the Greater Philadelphia Area. Vince's journey into bodywork began out of necessity. After years of battling unexplained chronic neck and shoulder pain, and facing surgery without a clear cause, he discovered Rolfing® Structural Integration as a last resort. It was a revelation. Not only did he become pain-free without surgery, but he also found his calling to help others.

Today, Vince uniquely blends the whole-body approach of Certified Rolfing with targeted orthopedic massage techniques. He doesn't just focus on where it hurts; he works with your entire myofascial system to find and address the root cause of your pain. His treatments are informed by the latest pain science and are paired with practical self-care education, empowering you with the tools to maintain your results long-term. Vince's ultimate goal is to facilitate your healing so effectively that you graduate from needing his care, becoming his best referral source.
